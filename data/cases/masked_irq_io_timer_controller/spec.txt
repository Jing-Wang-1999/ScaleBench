Behavioral Clarifications:
- The interrupt system uses both an icr register (7-bit interrupt flags, auto-set from ta/tb/alrm/keystrobe/ser_tx_irq each cycle unless overridden by write) and an imask register (7-bit mask); irq = |(icr & imask). Timer A and B initialize to 16'hFFFF on reset; Timer D initializes to 24'hFFFFFF. Timers reload from latch registers when expired or when irq_en is deasserted.
- `spmode` is tied to 0. The registers `ta_irq`, `tb_irq`, `d_irq`, `ta_irq_en`, `tb_irq_en`, and `d_irq_en` are never assigned in the reference, so they are unknown/unconstrained rather than zero. The timer latch registers are also not reset. Consequently, timer reload and interrupt behavior is not fully deterministic; this case is quarantined from model comparison.
