1. **Primary Function:**
   - The module performs a series of mathematical operations on the input `X`, generating multiple intermediate signals. These signals are accumulated in a chain of multiply-accumulate (MAC) units, ultimately producing the output `Y`.

2. **Control Flow:**
   - **Clock Triggered Operations:** The core sequential logic of this module is clock-triggered by `posedge clk` and controlled by `negedge reset`.
   - **Reset and Initialization:** When reset is active (`~reset`), all registers (MAC units) and the output `Y` are reset to zero. Once `reset` is de-asserted, the registers maintain their values until the next clock edge.

3. **Data Processing:**

   - **Intermediate Signal Calculation:** Each intermediate signal is calculated using combinations of additions and subtractions of shifted versions of `x1`, aiding in different mathematical needs.
   - **SCM Assignment:** Each intermediate signal is assigned to a corresponding SCM wire, `SCM_0` to `SCM_20`.
   - **MAC Accumulation:** In every clock cycle, the module updates the MAC registers (`MAC_0` to `MAC_20`) by adding respective SCM wires:
     - `MAC_0` accumulates `SCM_0`, then `MAC_1` accumulates the result from `MAC_0` and `SCM_1`, and the chain continues till `MAC_20`.
   - **Output Calculation:** The final output `Y` is assigned the value of `MAC_0`.

Include tasks `reset_reg` to clear registers and `init_reg` to maintain state during normal operation.
