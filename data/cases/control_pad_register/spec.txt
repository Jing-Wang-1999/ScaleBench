Implement a registered CPU control decoder with an active-low asynchronous reset.

Module Interface:
module control_pad_register (
    rd,
    zero,
    clk,
    rst_,
    opcode,
    sel,
    data_e,
    halt,
    inc_pc,
    ld_pc,
    ld_ac,
    ld_ir,
    wr
);

`opcode` is 3 bits wide. All other ports are one bit. The design contains three state registers named here as `state`, `phase`, and `history`. On the falling edge of `rst_`, all three registers become zero. On each rising edge of `clk`, update them simultaneously from the next-state equations below.

For the following equations, let `op0=opcode[0]`, `op1=opcode[1]`, `op2=opcode[2]`, `state` be the first register, `phase` the second register, and `history` the third register:

```
n1  = ~op0
n4  = zero
n7  = phase & history
n19 = state
n20 = ~n19 & ~history
n9  = n7 & ~n20
n8  = ~(op2 & op1)
n11 = state | n9
n5  = n11 & op1 & op2
n12 = phase & n20
n15 = ~(state & history & n4) | n8
n16 = op0 & op2 & op1

next_state   = n7 & ~n20
next_phase   = sel
next_history = ~(n19 & n4) | (state & phase)
```

Drive the outputs combinationally as follows:

```
wr     = ~(op0 | n5)
sel    = n9 | ~n20
data_e = ~(n8 | n9)
ld_ac  = n8 & n11
inc_pc = n12 | (n1 & ~(n5 | n15))
halt   = ~((op1 | op2) | (op0 & n12))
ld_pc  = n9 | n16
rd     = ~(n7 & (~state | history | n4))
ld_ir  = 1'b0
```
