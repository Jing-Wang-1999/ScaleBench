Behavioral Clarifications:
- Five separate posedge aclk / negedge aresetn always blocks handle AW, W, B, AR, and R channels. The write response (B channel) fires when s_axi_wready_reg, s_axi_wvalid, and s_axi_wlast are all asserted and s_axi_bvalid_reg is low. The read data channel returns 32'b0 (not DEADBEEF) and sets s_axi_rlast=1 when arready and arvalid are both high. Master outputs (m_axi_awaddr, etc.) are driven by continuous assign from slave inputs.
