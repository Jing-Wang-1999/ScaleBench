The `axi_register_slice` module provides an interface for AXI read and write transactions. It manages the flow of address, data, and responses by:

- **Handling read data (`R`) channel**: Responds to read requests with `s_axi_rvalid`, providing data through `s_axi_rdata`, finalizing transactions with `s_axi_rlast`.

Additionally, output signals to the master mirror the input slave signals to maintain coherency and protocol compliance, with master signals like `m_axi_awid`, `m_axi_awaddr`, `m_axi_wdata`, and `m_axi_arid` reflecting their respective slave counterparts.
