Implement an 8-bit clock-divider wrapper with four observable clock outputs.

Module Interface:
module clock_divider_output_taps (
    clock,
    reset,
    imem_clock,
    dmem_clock,
    processor_clock,
    regfile_clock
);

- `clock` is the rising-edge input clock.
- `reset` is an asynchronous active-high reset.
- Maintain an 8-bit register `clk_div`.
- On `reset`, set `clk_div` to `8'd0`.
- Otherwise, increment `clk_div` by one on every rising edge of `clock`, with normal 8-bit wraparound.
- Drive `imem_clock` from `clk_div[0]`.
- Drive both `dmem_clock` and `regfile_clock` from `clk_div[5]`.
- Drive `processor_clock` from `clk_div[7]`.

The four outputs are continuous views of the counter bits and have no additional gating or state.
