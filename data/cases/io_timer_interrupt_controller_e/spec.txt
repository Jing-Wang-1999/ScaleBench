Behavioral Clarifications:
- The interrupt system uses an 8-bit icr_reg (bits auto-updated from ta/tb/alrm/keystrobe/ser_tx_irq each cycle unless a write to icrs overrides bits) and an 8-bit int_en mask; irq = int_en[5:0] & icr_reg[5:0]. Timer registers (talo_reg, tblo_reg, tdlo_reg) are 16-bit and used in a non-standard way; the timer output signals (tmra_out, tmrb_out, tmrd_out) are derived from flag signals (ta, tb, alrm) rather than counter values.
- The icr_reg write behavior: if data_in[7] is set, the upper bits are OR-masked in; otherwise, lower bits are cleared. int_en: if data_in[7], bits are OR/AND-masked; otherwise, bits are cleared by data_in mask.
