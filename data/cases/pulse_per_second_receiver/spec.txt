Behavioral Clarifications:
- All internal registers initialize to zero except pps_status and up_pps_status_m and up_pps_status, which initialize to 1 (absent PPS). On rst, pps_status resets to 1'b1; on up_rstn=0, up_pps_status_m and up_pps_status reset to 1'b1.
- The pps_status flag is set to 1 (no PPS) when the free-running counter bit 28 goes high, indicating more than ~268M cycles since last PPS edge.
- The UP domain detects PPS toggle changes using XOR of adjacent bits in the 3-bit shift register, not a simple posedge detector.
