2. **Key Control Flow**: The operation is driven by the system clock (`sys_clk`) and reset signal (`sys_rst_n`).    - The module also manages features like MSI (Message Signaled Interrupts) through `msi_enable` and `msi_vector_width`.

4. **Clarity and Conciseness**:
   - Ensure that the internal logic effectively governs data transactions, configurations, and PCIe signal processing.
   - Handle AXI interaction: Ensure the correct management of ready/valid signals for data integrity and transaction correctness.
   - Include appropriate acknowledgment and response signals to indicate transaction completions.
