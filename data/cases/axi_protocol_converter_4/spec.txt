### Primary Function:

1. **Reset Logic**: On the assertion of the reset signal (`aresetn`), the module initializes or resets its internal states and output signals to default values.
2. **Clock Synchronization**: The entire operation is driven by the clock signal (`aclk`), ensuring synchronization of AXI transactions.

   - It asserts `s_axi_wready` when ready to receive the data and then transmits it to the master interface (`m_axi_wdata`, `m_axi_wvalid`, etc.).

3. **Write Response Channel**:
   - The module captures the write response from the master (`m_axi_bresp`, `m_axi_bvalid`) and forwards this response to the slave interface (`s_axi_bresp`, `s_axi_bvalid`).

4. **Read Address Channel**:
   - When `s_axi_arvalid` is asserted, the module captures the read address (`s_axi_araddr`) and other attributes, asserting `s_axi_arready` once it is ready to accept the address.
   - The address and other signals are then sent to the master interface (`m_axi_araddr`, `m_axi_arvalid`, etc.).

5. **Read Data Channel**:
   - The read data and attributes from the master interface (`m_axi_rdata`, `m_axi_rvalid`) are captured and then forwarded to the slave interface (`s_axi_rdata`, `s_axi_rvalid`).
